Role

01

Data Intelligence & List Building

  • List Building & Scraping

    Building highly targeted databases of companies and specific decision-makers using data mining platforms.

  • Data Cleansing & Validation

    Verifying that email addresses are active and phone numbers are direct to avoid high bounce rates and protect email domain health.

  • Lead Segmentation

    Categorizing lists by industry, company size, geography, or job seniority so that marketing messages can be personalized.

02

Outbound Strategy & Execution (Push)

  • Cold Email Management

    Writing, scheduling, and running automated email sequences that feel personalized and personal.

  • Social Selling Sourcing

    Engaging with prospects on platforms like LinkedIn by identifying trigger events (e.g., a prospect getting promoted) to open conversations.

  • Multi-Channel Sequencing

    Coordinating touchpoints so a prospect receives an email, gets a LinkedIn view, and receives a soft phone call over a structured 14-day window.

03

Inbound Traffic Conversion (Pull)

  • Lead Magnet Promotion

    Deploying and marketing high-value assets that require an email address to access.

  • Landing Page Optimization

    Partnering with copywriters and designers to ensure that web pages built for ads have highly visible forms and compelling call-to-actions.

  • Form Logic Management

    Working to ensure that forms ask for just enough information to qualify a lead without making the form so long that prospects give up.

04

Funnel Operations, Routing & Analytics

  • Lead Enrichment Collaboration

    Coordinating with the GTM Engineer to automatically enrich thin leads.

  • Lead Routing & Handoff

    Setting up validation rules so that qualified leads are pushed immediately into the CRM and cleanly assigned to the correct sales rep without delay.

  • Performance Reporting

    Tracking, analyzing, and presenting data on critical metrics: Cost Per Lead (CPL), Form Conversion Rates, and Lead-to-Opportunity conversion velocity.
